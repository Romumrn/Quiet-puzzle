## English (en-US) — default

**App name**

```
Quiet Puzzle: Calm Blocks
```

**Short description**

```
Slide pastel blocks to their coloured doors. A calm puzzle, 1000 levels.
```

**Full description**

```
Quiet Puzzle is a calm block puzzle for the end of a long day.

No opponent, no countdown to panic over. Just a grid, soft pastel blocks and coloured doors on the walls. Drag a block, and it glides out when it reaches a door of its colour that it fits through. Simple to pick up, satisfying to master.

♪ EVERY MOVE RINGS A NOTE
Each block that leaves rings a chime, one step higher than the last — a good run of moves turns into a little melody. The music is played on real instruments: piano, harp, hang drum, kalimba and water glasses.

🌸 50 WORLDS, 1000 LEVELS
Travel from cherry blossom pink through wisteria, eucalyptus, autumn maple and winter frost. Every world has its own colours, its own branch on the map — and every level is proven solvable.

🧩 A NEW IDEA IN EVERY WORLD
Blocks on rails, sealed blocks, locks that count down, jokers, anchors, gates with a capacity, heavy blocks, two-colour blocks, a key that opens every lock, one-way cells… The rules stay simple; the puzzles get deeper.

📅 A NEW PUZZLE EVERY DAY
The same daily grid for everyone, with a leaderboard — and one day in two, a grid created by a player.

✏️ BUILD YOUR OWN LEVELS
The level editor checks your grid with an exact solver, tells you how many moves it takes, and lets you submit it to the community.

💡 HELP WHEN YOU WANT IT
The bulb never guesses: it searches the board you are playing and shows you the next move. Undo, restart and the hammer are there too.

🌿 PLAY YOUR WAY
• Relaxed clock: a win always counts, fewer moves earn more stars
• Daily quests, streaks, and a flower that blooms one petal per level won
• Plays offline, no account needed
• Sign in with Google to keep your progress across devices
• Colour-blind mode: every colour gets its own pattern
• English, French, Spanish, Italian, Chinese

Free to play, with optional rewarded videos. No in-app purchases.

Take a breath, slide a block, and let the board clear.
```

## 中文（简体）(zh-CN)

**应用名称**

```
Quiet Puzzle：静心方块
```

**简短说明**

```
把粉彩方块滑到同色的门。治愈系解谜，1000 个关卡。
```

**完整说明**

```
Quiet Puzzle 是一款治愈系方块解谜游戏，适合在漫长的一天之后放松身心。

没有对手，没有让人紧张的倒计时。只有一张棋盘、柔和的粉彩方块和墙上的彩色门。拖动方块，当它来到同色且宽度足够的门前，就会轻轻滑出棋盘。上手简单，越玩越有味道。

♪ 每一步都是一个音符
每个离开棋盘的方块都会响起一声风铃，比上一声高一个音阶——连贯的操作会汇成一段小旋律。背景音乐由真实乐器演奏：钢琴、竖琴、手碟、卡林巴琴和水杯琴。

🌸 50 个世界，1000 个关卡
从樱花粉一路走到紫藤、桉树、秋日枫叶与冬日霜雪。每个世界都有自己的配色和地图上的枝叶，每一关都保证有解。

🧩 每个世界都有新玩法
滑轨方块、封印方块、倒计时锁、百搭方块、锚点、限量通过的门、重型方块、双色方块、打开所有锁的钥匙、单向格子……规则始终简单，谜题越来越有深度。

📅 每天一道新谜题
所有玩家同一张每日棋盘，并设有排行榜；每两天中有一天，会是玩家亲手设计的关卡。

✏️ 创作你自己的关卡
关卡编辑器用精确求解器检查你的棋盘，告诉你需要几步完成，并可提交给社区。

💡 需要时才有提示
灯泡从不乱猜：它会在当前棋盘上搜索，并为你演示下一步。还有撤销、重来和锤子。

🌿 按自己的节奏玩
• 宽松的计时：胜利永远算数，步数越少星星越多
• 每日任务、连续登录奖励，以及每赢一关就绽放一片花瓣的小花
• 支持离线游玩，无需账号
• 使用 Google 登录，在所有设备上同步进度
• 色盲模式：每种颜色都有专属图案
• 支持中文、英语、法语、西班牙语、意大利语

免费游玩，可选择观看奖励视频。无应用内购买。

深呼吸，滑动方块，让棋盘慢慢清空。
```
