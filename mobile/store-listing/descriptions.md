## English (en-US) — default

**App name**

```
Quiet Puzzle: Calm Blocks
```

**Short description**

```
A gentle puzzle to slow down. Slide a block, hear a note, breathe out.
```

**Full description**

```
It's late. The day was long. You want to play something, just not something that shouts at you.

Quiet Puzzle is a grid, a few pastel blocks and some coloured doors around the edges. Slide a block to a door of its colour and it glides away. Then the next one. Little by little the board empties, and so does your head.

No timer racing you, no opponent, no music pushing you along. You look, you try something, you get it wrong, you undo, you try again. And then, all at once, you see it. That small click when the solution appears is what this game is made of.

Every block that leaves plays a note, a little higher than the one before. String a few good moves together and you've played a tiny melody without meaning to. The background music is recorded on real instruments: piano, harp, hang drum, kalimba and water glasses.

What's inside

• 1000 levels across 50 worlds, from cherry blossom to winter frost, by way of wisteria, eucalyptus and autumn maple
• A new idea in every world: blocks on rails, locks and keys, doors that only let so many through, one-way cells… The rules stay simple. The thinking gets deeper.
• Every single level has been checked by a solver. There is always a way out.
• A puzzle of the day, the same for everyone, and every other day it was designed by a player
• An editor to build your own grids and share them
• A bulb for when you're truly stuck. It doesn't guess: it searches your board and shows you the next move.
• A flower that opens one petal for every level you clear

The small things that matter

• Plays offline, no account needed
• Sign in with Google if you want your progress on another device
• Colour-blind mode: every colour has its own pattern
• English, French, Spanish, Italian and Chinese
• Free, with ads. No in-app purchases.

Five minutes on the bus, half an hour before bed, a slow Sunday under a blanket. Quiet Puzzle was made for those moments.

Breathe. Slide a block. Let the board clear.
```

## 中文（简体）(zh-CN)

**应用名称**

```
Quiet Puzzle：静心方块
```

**简短说明**

```
一款让你慢下来的温柔解谜。滑动方块，听一个音符，深呼吸。
```

**完整说明**

```
夜深了，这一天很长。你想玩点什么，但不想要吵闹的游戏。

Quiet Puzzle 只有一张棋盘、几块粉彩色的方块，和四周的彩色门。把方块滑到同色的门前，它就会轻轻离开。然后是下一块。棋盘一点点变空，脑子也跟着安静下来。

没有追着你跑的倒计时，没有对手，也没有催促你的音乐。你观察，尝试，走错，撤销，再来。然后忽然之间，你看懂了。答案浮现的那一下“咔哒”，就是这个游戏的全部。

每个离开的方块都会奏出一个音符，比上一个稍高一点。连着走对几步，你就不经意间弹出了一小段旋律。背景音乐由真实乐器录制：钢琴、竖琴、手碟、卡林巴琴和水杯琴。

你会遇到

• 50 个世界，1000 个关卡，从樱花一路走到冬日霜雪，途经紫藤、桉树和秋天的枫叶
• 每个世界都有新点子：滑轨方块、锁与钥匙、只能通过有限方块的门、单向格子……规则始终简单，思考越来越深。
• 每一关都经过求解器验证，一定有解。
• 每日谜题，所有人同一题；每两天就有一天，是由玩家设计的
• 关卡编辑器，创作并分享你自己的棋盘
• 真的卡住时，还有灯泡。它不会乱猜，而是在你的棋盘上搜索，告诉你下一步。
• 每通过一关，小花就多开一片花瓣

那些重要的小细节

• 支持离线游玩，无需注册
• 想在其他设备上继续，可以用 Google 登录
• 色盲模式：每种颜色都有自己的图案
• 支持中文、英语、法语、西班牙语和意大利语
• 免费游玩，含广告。无应用内购买。

地铁上的五分钟，睡前的半小时，窝在被子里的周日午后。Quiet Puzzle 就是为这些时刻而做的。

深呼吸。滑动一块方块。让棋盘慢慢清空。
```
